Privacy Policy for Fazlook

Effective date: March 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Fazlook ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it through this Privacy Policy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our app available on the Google Play Store.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information to provide and improve our services:

  • Account & Login Information: We use Firebase Authentication to allow you to log in using Google Play Games or Game Center. Your account information, such as username, email (if provided), and unique ID, may be stored.
  • Game Data: Leaderboards, scores, achievements, and in-game progress.
  • Cloud Data: Saved game data stored using Unity Cloud Save and Firebase Cloud.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for app functionality and analytics.
  • Usage Information: How you interact with the app, features you use, and crash reports (for debugging).

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide and maintain the app functionality (e.g., login, leaderboards, cloud save).
  • Store and sync your game progress across devices.
  • Analyze usage to improve the app and fix bugs.
  • Prevent abuse and detect unauthorized access.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service Providers: Firebase, Unity, Google Play Games, and Apple Game Center to enable app features.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect rights.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Your Choices

  • You can choose not to log in with Google Play Games or Game Center.
  • You may request to delete your account by contacting us at app@2oolameme.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
