How to request deletion

Email: app@2oolameme.com

Send us an email from any address and include:

  • The game's name: Fazlook
  • Your in-game username (shown on your profile), and the account you sign in with (Google Play Games or Game Center)
  • Your device type (optional, helps us find your account)

What happens next

  • We confirm the request and delete your account within 14 days.
  • We email you when the deletion is complete.

Data we delete

  • Your account and profile (username, and the sign-in identifiers from Google Play Games or Game Center)
  • Your game progress, scores, leaderboard entries and saved game data
  • Any other personal information linked to your account

Data we may keep

  • Records we must keep by law (for example, purchase records), only for as long as the law requires
